So, pick one

You do not have to weigh all of that. Here is the short version, in the order I would choose.

  1. Riviera Maya. If the experience is the point. Only one where we leave Midway in the morning and are in the water that afternoon.
  2. Aruba. If you want zero weather anxiety. Outside the hurricane belt, cheapest of the good ones, we just pay for our own dinners.
  3. Panama. If price is the point and you do not mind roughing the travel.
  4. Cayo Espanto. Not now. Let's save this one and do it right in February.
